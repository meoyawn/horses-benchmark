Field notes

Thoroughbred · Equus ferus caballus

Form

Follow the crest from the poll between the ears down to the withers above the shoulder. From the side, the topline dips gently behind the withers before rising over the hindquarters.

Coat and markings

The bay has a brown body with a dark mane, tail and lower legs. Switch to chestnut to see warmer points, or grey to reveal dappling across the barrel. The star on the forehead and the hind sock identify this individual in each coat.

Reading the gait

At a walk, the hooves land in four separate beats. In trot, the diagonal pairs move together. Slow the playback and follow the hoof contact display to see the rhythm.

This model, its markings and its motion were built procedurally for Equine Atlas in Three.js.
